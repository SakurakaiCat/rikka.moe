<div class="ai-summary">
Secondary Math Journey is a long LaTeX manuscript distilled from three years of high-school math notes. Its main idea is to move from pattern matching to tracing the logical path behind a problem. The project now lives on the standalone book site [b.rikka.moe](https://b.rikka.moe); this page is kept as an English overview and archive entry.

    <h2>Why I wrote it</h2>
    <p>The manuscript began as Obsidian notes I kept while studying mathematics in high school, and was later typeset in LaTeX. I wanted a book that did more than list problem types and worked examples.</p>
    <p>Instead of asking readers to recognise a template and copy a solution, the book tries to show how a result grows from definitions, theorems, branches of reasoning, and finally a converging answer.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="akari-books-card">
    <p class="akari-books-kicker">Status</p>
    <h2>Current state</h2>
    <p>The project is paused indefinitely. Maintaining a very large manuscript takes long uninterrupted blocks of time, and the practical return has been too low to make that sustainable right now.</p>
    <p>I may eventually migrate parts of the material into software and explore multilingual versions, but the current manuscript may remain the final full version for quite a while.</p>
